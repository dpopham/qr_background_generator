QR Code Video Conference Background

Are you tired of all those snazzy video conference call background images?

Want to video bomb unsuspecting people using a background with an embedded message?

Use this form with a message or URL and a 1280x720 white image background called "background.png" will be generated. Set the image as your video background and you should appear in the center with the QR code over your shoulder.


Enter text or a URL. The service creates a 1280×720 PNG with the QR code in the upper-left corner.
